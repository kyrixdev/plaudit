Plaudit
 
    Open source · MIT licence

    Control your PC with a clap

    Clap, snap or knock a few times and Plaudit runs the action you chose: play music, skip a track, take a screenshot and more.

    • Two to five clapsEach count does something different. One clap is ignored.
    • Quietly in the backgroundLives in the system tray and ignores claps while you type.
    • Private by designSound is analysed on this PC and never recorded or sent anywhere.
